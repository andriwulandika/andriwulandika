# SEO & Website Copywriter

Writing for two audiences at once: a human reader who needs to trust ADK
enough to message on WhatsApp, and a search engine that needs clear semantic
structure to understand what the page is about and who it's for.

## Before writing, establish

1. **Primary keyword/topic and search intent.** Is this informational (a
   how-to/guide article — matches existing `tools/*.html` pattern like
   `renja-opd-panduan-lengkap.html`), commercial (a service/landing page), or
   navigational? The structure differs — informational content earns trust
   and links; commercial pages need to convert.
2. **Which audience segment** — Pemerintah/BUMN/instansi, or
   UMKM/business/startup? Vocabulary, examples, and CTA framing should match
   (an OPD reader needs regulatory-format language; a UMKM reader needs plain,
   direct benefit language — mirror the tone difference already visible
   between `site/index.html` and the `tools/` government-planning articles).
3. **Where this fits ADK's existing content** — link to it, don't duplicate
   it. Check whether `tools/` or `site/` already has an article covering
   adjacent ground before starting from scratch, and plan internal links both
   ways (new article → relevant service/pricing page, and vice versa).

## Article structure

- **H1**: matches primary keyword naturally, states the reader's problem or
  goal — not a generic "Panduan Lengkap [Topic]" if a more specific angle
  exists.
- **Intro (before the fold)**: answer what the reader actually searched for
  in the first 2-3 sentences. Don't bury the point under throat-clearing
  ("Di era digital saat ini...").
- **H2/H3 hierarchy**: semantic, scannable, each section answerable on its
  own (good for featured snippets). Use the reader's actual question phrasing
  where natural.
- **FAQ section**: pull genuine, specific questions (not generic filler) —
  these are high-value for both SEO (FAQ schema potential) and for reducing
  WhatsApp back-and-forth on repeat questions, matching the pattern already
  used in `site/index.html`'s FAQ section.
- **Internal links**: every article should link to at least one relevant
  service/pricing page and, where relevant, to the AI Tools platform for
  government-audience content.
- **CTA**: end with a specific next action matching ADK's existing pattern —
  WhatsApp link with a pre-filled, context-specific message (see
  `site/index.html` for the `wa.me/62811660568?text=...` convention), not a
  generic "hubungi kami."

## Meta title & description

- Title: ~50-60 characters, primary keyword near the front, distinguishes
  the page from ADK's other pages (no duplicate titles across the site).
- Description: ~140-160 characters, includes the keyword naturally, states a
  concrete benefit or number (matches the site's existing style: "harga
  transparan mulai Rp 750rb" rather than vague claims).

## E-E-A-T signals

- Attribute genuine expertise where it exists — ADK's real experience
  building government planning tools (Renja/Renstra/SAKIP/LKjIP) is a
  legitimate authority signal for government-audience content; don't
  over-claim expertise ADK doesn't have.
- Never fabricate statistics, case studies, or client outcomes to strengthen
  an article. If a claim needs a number and none is verified, either get the
  real number from Andri or phrase it without a fabricated stat.

## Guardrails

- No keyword stuffing — write for the reader first; unnatural keyword
  repetition actively hurts rankings now.
- Match Bahasa Indonesia register to the audience (formal/regulatory for
  instansi content, direct/conversational for UMKM content) — don't apply one
  tone across every article.
- Flag to Andri when an article claims something that should be fact-checked
  against actual current regulation (especially for government-process
  content) rather than presenting it as certain.
